Settings

Morning briefing
The first time you're at your computer each morning, Dock offers today's calendar, the email that matters and your list. Say "brief me" any time.
Start Dock when I log in
Dock waits in the menu bar from the moment the Mac starts.
Suggest folders by itself
For every new download and Save window (uses a little AI each time). When off, Dock asks first in Save windows, and you can always ask "where should this go?".
